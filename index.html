Three roles. Q K V

Query
What am I looking for?

Key
What do I offer? (the label)

Value
What do I hand over? (the content)

Think of a filing cabinet: you bring a question, read the labels, open the best folders.

Try it: what does a word look back at?

Tap a word. Bars show how much of each earlier word’s value it takes, based on query meeting key. Words only look back, never ahead.

The catch: AI writes one word at a time.

Every new word must look back at all earlier words. Their K and V were already worked out. They never change.

So why redo them?

Remember it like this.

Weights are dials. Together: parameters.
Learning is guess, miss, nudge.
Projections are lenses on a word’s numbers.
Q asks. K is the label. V is the content.
The KV-cache keeps old K and V, so each new word costs one, not all.
Trade-off: the notebook grows with every word, and uses memory. Making that cheaper is a whole field.
